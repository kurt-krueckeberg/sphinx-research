# Was Anna Maria Vahlsing previously married to Friedrich Kuhlmann or Joh. Tönnies Eggerding?

**Target Subject:** Anna Maria Vahlsing, wife of Johann Henrich Krückeberg

**Location:** Petzen parish, County of Schaumburg-Lippe (Berenbusch / Evesen / Höckersau)

**Objective:** Determine how Johann Henrich Krückeberg acquired *Brinksitzerstelle* No. 10 in Berenbusch through his 1727 marriage to Anna Maria Vahlsing.

## I. Research Question

> **Was Anna Maria Vahlsing previously married to Friedrich Kuhlmann or Joh. Tönnies Eggerding, thereby providing the connection through which Johann Henrich Krückeberg acquired *Brinksitzerstelle* No. 10 when he married her on 28 September 1727?**

## II. Known Evidence

1. In the 1743–1744 *Generallandesvisitation*, Johann Henrich Krückeberg stated that he acquired Berenbusch No. 10 by marrying onto the holding (*„habe darauf geheyrahtet“*). Friedrich Kuhlmann and Joh. Tönnies Eggerding are identified as previous holders.

2. Johann Henrich Krückeberg of Berenbusch married **Anna Maria Vahlsing of Evesen on 28 September 1727**.

3. Anna Maria Vahlsing has now been independently identified in the Petzen church records. Her baptism entry reads:

   > *das Scheper auf der Höltersau. Töchterlein, Anna Maria, getauffet, den 4 Maji, gevatter das Schefers seine Schwester*

   The wording closely parallels the 1701 baptism of her older sister Anna Elisabeth Vahlsing:

   > *Das Schepers auf der Höckersau, Töchterlein, Anna Elisabeth, getauft den 19 Junÿ ...*

4. Confirmation records further distinguish the two sisters: **Anna Elisabeth was confirmed in 1713 and Anna Maria in 1716**. Anna Maria can therefore be treated as a distinct individual rather than as an alternate name for Anna Elisabeth.

5. The father is described in the baptismal records as *Scheper/Schefer*, Low German forms corresponding to standard German *Schäfer* (“shepherd”). No evidence presently connects him directly with Berenbusch No. 10.

## III. Working Hypotheses

### Hypothesis A
Anna Maria Vahlsing married **Friedrich Kuhlmann** before 1727 and became connected with Berenbusch No. 10 through that marriage. After Kuhlmann's death, she married Johann Henrich Krückeberg, who thereby succeeded to the holding.

### Hypothesis B
Anna Maria Vahlsing married **Joh. Tönnies Eggerding** before 1727 and became connected with Berenbusch No. 10 through that marriage. After Eggerding's death, she married Johann Henrich Krückeberg, who thereby succeeded to the holding.

## IV. Research Steps

1. **Search the Petzen marriage register** from approximately 1718 through 28 September 1727 for a marriage of Anna Maria Vahlsing to Friedrich Kuhlmann or Joh. Tönnies Eggerding.

2. **Search the Petzen burial register**, concentrating on the years immediately preceding September 1727, for the death of Friedrich Kuhlmann or Joh. Tönnies Eggerding.

3. If a prior marriage is found, **search the relevant NLA Bückeburg administrative or estate records** for Berenbusch No. 10 around the date of the husband's death and Anna Maria's remarriage to determine how the holding passed to Johann Henrich Krückeberg.

4. **Only if these focused searches fail**, broaden the investigation to identify another explanation for Anna Maria's connection with No. 10.

## V. Expected Result

The research should determine whether Johann Henrich Krückeberg's statement *„habe darauf geheyrahtet“* reflects his marriage to the widow of one of the preceding holders of Berenbusch No. 10 and, if so, identify that previous husband and the resulting succession mechanism.
