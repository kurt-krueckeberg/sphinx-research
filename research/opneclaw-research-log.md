# Arcinsys research log: Krückeberg family, Berenbusch area before 1850

Investigation date: 5 August 2026  
Archive: Niedersächsisches Landesarchiv, Abteilung Bückeburg (NLA BU)  
Starting point: https://www.arcinsys.niedersachsen.de/arcinsys/detailAction.action?detailid=a2

## Method and scope

I opened the live Arcinsys archive record, inspected the catalog/search interface, browsed indexed classification pages, opened record and fonds descriptions, and ran controlled site searches against Arcinsys. Every archival assertion in the final report comes from an Arcinsys page located during this investigation. Search-engine results were used only as a discovery layer for Arcinsys pages; titles, dates, references, descriptions, classifications, and representation status were taken from Arcinsys records.

Arcinsys terminology learned and used:

- **Bestand** = fonds/record group; **Gliederung (Klassifikation)** = catalog branch; **Verzeichnung** = item-level description.
- **Signatur** = archival reference; **Laufzeit** = date range; **Nebenlaufzeit** = subsidiary dates; **Enthält** = scope/content note; **Alte Archivsignatur** = former reference.
- Index groups include **GEOB** (place), **PERS** (person), **SACH** (subject), and **Institution**.
- **Original – Akte** and microfilm/fiche or security-film representations are analog. Only a representation explicitly labeled **Nutzungsdigitalisat** with “Digitalisat vorhanden” was treated as digitized. None of the priority pre-1850 files below showed such a label.

## Search outcomes and negative evidence

- Exact family-name searches produced three high-value Berenbusch records: K 2 A Nr. 1291 (1817–1818), K 2 A Nr. 689 (1841–1845), and L 120a D Nr. 174 (1849).
- Variant searching located the catalog-attested form **Krückenberg** in Dep. 59 Nr. 167, but its Hessisch Oldendorf context makes it only a possible collateral/migration lead.
- Broad searches for local military, emigration, poor relief, and guild files did not yield a clearly Berenbusch-specific pre-1850 item title. The most useful actual movement record located was L 2 A Nr. 16 (Abzugsgeld, 1651–1736); the best occupation/citizenship lead was Dep. 59 Nr. 1151 (1814–1819), which names a Krückeberg but belongs to Hessisch Oldendorf.
- No priority pre-1850 item located showed a **Nutzungsdigitalisat** representation. The repeated Arcinsys representation pattern is Original plus Sicherungsfilm and sometimes fiche; those require archive access/order, even though catalog metadata are online.
