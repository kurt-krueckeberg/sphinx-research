## Executive findings

Investigation completed: 5 August 2026  
Archive: Niedersächsisches Landesarchiv, Abteilung Bückeburg (NLA BU)

The Arcinsys catalog contains a coherent, unusually strong documentary
chain for a **Colon Krückeberg at Berenbusch house/farm no. 10**:

1. **1817–1818:** land was allotted to Colon Krückeberg no. 10 (K 2 A Nr. 1291).
2. **1841–1845:** the Hofkammer lent him 200 Reichstaler (K 2 A Nr. 689).
3. **1849:** his farm was voluntarily sold (L 120a D Nr. 174).

Those three files are the first order priority. They can plausibly contain given names, signatures,
residence and status, descriptions of land, obligations, purchase/sale terms, creditors, relatives
or heirs, and earlier title evidence.

The strongest surname-independent sources are the **1732 population tables**, the **1731–1748
householder/parish list**, the **1737 Amt Bückeburg house-and-farm-owner list**, and the **1734–1745
surveys of Evesen, Berenbusch and Nordholz**. Together they may identify the predecessor household
attached to the later no. 10 farm even if the surname changed through marriage or farm-name usage.

None of the priority pre-1850 item pages displayed an online-use digital copy. They show analog
originals, security film, and sometimes microfiche; they therefore appear to require ordering or
consultation at NLA Bückeburg.

## G. Catalog structure and related sections to investigate next

Arcinsys’s own L 2 fonds description warns that old titles were filed by initial letter and can be
misleading; similar subjects are scattered. A complete on-site/request strategy should therefore
combine classification browsing with register volumes and not rely on title-word searching.

Priority related branches:

- **L 2 – Alte Regierungsregistratur (1553–1823):** continue the full four pages of *Landesvermessung und Statistik*; inspect any surviving Lagerbücher, Messregister, house-numbering, tax/Schatz/Kontribution, military and poor-relief classifications.
- **K 2 – Jüngeres Kammerarchiv:** inspect neighboring numbers around A 689 and A 1291 and the Berenbusch **Bauerngüter und Kolonien** classification. The L 120a fonds description explicitly points to K 2 for supplementary Bauerngut data.
- **L 120a – Justizamt Bückeburg:** inspect court/register series for contracts, voluntary jurisdiction, guardianship, estates, marriage settlements, debts and land sales. Not all older machine-catalog entries have index terms.
- **L 101a – Amt Bückeburg:** inspect register, Schatz/Kontribution, services/dues, domanial, village-police, residence and poor-relief series. It is the lower authority most likely to contain whole-community administrative lists.
- **L 20 – Justizkanzlei Bückeburg:** appellate or higher-jurisdiction cases and guardianship/inheritance matters; a named Arcinsys parallel to L 120a.
- **L 121a – Amtsgericht Bückeburg** and **L 122a – Stadtgericht Bückeburg:** successor/parallel court records, especially cases begun before institutional changes.
- **H 1 – Regierung Grafschaft Schaumburg**, **F 2 – Fürstliches Hausarchiv**, and **L 9 – Verordnungen:** Arcinsys names these as parallel or complementary to L 2; use for pre-1647 government, displaced central records, and record-keeping mandates.
- **Dep. 9 A/B/R – Stadt Bückeburg:** citizenship, residents, occupations/guilds, poor relief, tax and municipal accounts, if evidence shows a move from Berenbusch into the city. Fonds-level descriptions say these catalog groups are digitized, but item-level digital availability must be confirmed.
